Fridge Wiring Editor

Components

Click or drag a component onto the canvas.
Wires: click any port (blue dot) or press W, click to add bends, finish on a port, double-click or Enter. Hold Alt for diagonal segments.
Double-click a wire to add a bend, double-click a bend to remove it, drag bends to move them.
R rotate · H/V mirror · Del delete
Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+C/V copy/paste · Ctrl+Z/Y undo/redo
Drag on empty canvas to box-select; Shift+click adds to the selection. Editing a field with several parts selected changes all of them.
